Services Catalogue & Test Prerequisites

Explore our expanded diagnostic tests and review mandatory preparation guidelines before your visit.

Ultrasound & Color Doppler

~25 mins

Abdomen, Pelvis, Obstetric anomaly scans, and vascular studies.

Prerequisite / Preparation: • Upper Abdomen: 6 hours strict fasting required.
• Pelvic / Obstetric: Full urinary bladder (drink 4-5 glasses of water 1 hour prior).

CT Scan (Computed Tomography)

~35 mins

Brain, Chest, Abdomen, and High-Resolution Angiography.

Prerequisite / Preparation: • Contrast CT: Serum Creatinine blood test report mandatory.
• 4-hour fasting required for abdominal contrast studies.
• Remove all metallic objects.

MRI Scan

~50 mins

Brain, Spine, Joints, and Soft Tissue high-field imaging.

Prerequisite / Preparation: • Strictly NO metallic objects, cards, or jewellery.
• Inform staff of pacemakers, metallic implants, or severe claustrophobia.

Digital X-Ray

~15 mins

Chest, Spine, Extremities, and Orthopedic radiography.

Prerequisite / Preparation: • No special preparation required.
• Remove necklaces, buttons, or metal zippers near the scan area.

Mammography

~20 mins

Low-dose digital breast screening for early detection.

Prerequisite / Preparation: • Do not apply deodorant, talcum powder, or lotion on the day of the test.
• Bring previous mammogram films if available.

ECG & Heart Screening

~10 mins

12-lead resting electrocardiogram.

Prerequisite / Preparation: • Wear comfortable two-piece clothing.
• Avoid applying heavy body oils or creams on the chest area.
